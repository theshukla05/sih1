Section 1: Statutory Framework

Institutional Mandate & Regulatory Jurisdiction

Succeeding the historic Indian Standards Institution (ISI founded 1947), BIS stands as the National Standards Body of India under the statutory mandate of the BIS Act, 2016.

BIS Act, 2016

Primary Statutory Body

Empowered by parliamentary statute to enforce compulsory conformity assessment, penalize counterfeit marks, and declare mandatory Indian Standards.

Consumer Affairs

Nodal Ministry

Operating under the Department of Consumer Affairs, ensuring strict protection of consumer rights and harmonized food & product safety regimes.

ISO & IEC Member

International Body

Representing the Republic of India at the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) and International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC).

SIH26107 Protocol

Hackathon Architecture

Next-generation automated verification pipeline and cryptographic ledger integrity for seamless citizen compliance and real-time validation.

Understanding the 3 Marks of Genuine Hallmarked Gold

Under BIS statutory regulations, every gold jewelry article must carry three distinct laser-engraved identification marks to guarantee purity and protect consumers against adulteration.

1

BIS Standard Mark

Triangular official hallmark symbol confirming compliance with IS 1417 standards.

2

Purity in Carat and Fineness

22K916 (91.6% pure), 18K750 (75.0% pure), or 14K585 (58.5% pure gold).

3

6-Digit Alphanumeric HUID

Hallmark Unique Identification: A unique cryptographic serial code laser-engraved onto each individual piece at Assaying Centres.

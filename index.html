Section 1: Statutory Framework

Institutional Mandate & Regulatory Jurisdiction

Succeeding the historic Indian Standards Institution (ISI, established 1947), the Bureau of Indian Standards operates under parliamentary mandate as the National Standards Body of India (BIS Act, 2016).

BIS Act, 2016

Statutory Core

Statutory authority to declare mandatory Indian Standards, certify industrial conformity, and penalize unauthorized use of standard marks.

Consumer Affairs

Nodal Ministry

Operates under Department of Consumer Affairs, aligning statutory consumer protection frameworks with mandatory quality control regimes.

ISO & IEC Member

Global Standing

Founding member of ISO and official Indian National Committee for IEC, representing India in international standard harmonization.

e-BIS Unified Digital

Digital Governance

Complete digital lifecycle tracking: online licensing, surveillance audits, mobile scanning via BIS Care, and real-time laboratory workflows.

The 3 Marks of Genuine Hallmarked Gold

Under BIS statutory regulations, every piece of gold jewelry must carry three distinct laser-engraved identification marks to guarantee purity and protect consumers against adulteration.

1

BIS Triangular Standard Mark

Confirms article testing at an Assaying & Hallmarking Centre according to IS 1417.

2

Purity in Carats & Fineness

Explicitly stamped: 22K916 (91.6% pure), 18K750 (75.0% pure), or 14K585 (58.5% pure).

3

6-Digit Alphanumeric HUID

Hallmark Unique Identification: A distinct serial laser-engraved code for absolute chain-of-custody.
